Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

How long does a typical repair take?

Timing depends on the issue and the parts needed. Submit a ticket with your device and concern so the shop can advise you.

Do you offer a warranty for repairs?

If your repair has a warranty, its end date appears in the dashboard Warranty Tracker. Contact the shop to confirm coverage for your repair.

Can I track my order or repair status?

Yes. Open Orders or Repairs to see your records and their current status.

What payment methods do you accept?

The current checkout offers GCash, Maribank, BPI, and RCBC. Keep your payment reference and receipt for verification.

Do you offer home service or pickup/delivery?

Order checkout offers pickup or delivery. For home service or other arrangements, submit a ticket or contact the shop before booking.
